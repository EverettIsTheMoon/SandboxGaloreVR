LEFT-HAND RACER

VR (left hand): reach out and squeeze grip near the wheel to grab it, then move your hand around the wheel to turn it - your hand stays locked on the rim until you let go (the wheel re-centers on release) · grab the lever beside the wheel and push it forward = Drive, pull back = Reverse · trigger = gas · stick back = brake · stick click = recenter · X = get out/in of car (stick walks) · Y = repair (hold 1s = menu) · hand tracking: pinch index = gas, pinch middle = grab, pinch ring = brake
Keyboard: W gas · S brake · A/D steer · R reverse lever · X repair · E get out/in
